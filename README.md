# 🛒 DMart Retail Sales Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **DMart Retail Sales Analytics Dashboard** is an end-to-end **Business Intelligence** project developed using **Power BI**. The goal of this project is to transform raw retail sales data into meaningful business insights through interactive dashboards. It enables business users to monitor sales performance, customer behavior, product profitability, and store operations in real time.

The project demonstrates the complete BI workflow, including **data cleaning, data transformation, data modeling, DAX calculations, KPI creation, and interactive dashboard development**.

---

# 🎯 Project Purpose

The primary purpose of this project is to help business stakeholders make informed decisions by providing a centralized dashboard that tracks key retail performance metrics.

This dashboard helps answer business questions such as:

* Which products generate the highest revenue and profit?
* Which customers contribute the most to total sales?
* Which product categories perform the best?
* How do monthly sales and profits change over time?
* Which store locations perform better than others?
* What are the current business KPIs?

---

# 💼 Business Use Case

Retail companies generate large volumes of sales data every day. Analyzing this data manually is time-consuming and inefficient.

This dashboard enables managers and decision-makers to:

* Monitor overall business performance
* Track Revenue, Profit, Orders, and Customers
* Identify top-performing products and brands
* Understand customer purchasing behavior
* Compare store performance across different locations
* Analyze monthly sales trends for forecasting and planning
* Make faster, data-driven business decisions

# 📊 Dashboard Pages

## 1️⃣ Executive Performance Dashboard

The **Executive Performance Dashboard** provides a high-level overview of the organization's overall business performance. It enables decision-makers to monitor key performance indicators (KPIs) and identify revenue and profit trends over time.

### Key Features
- Total Revenue, Total Profit, Total Orders, and Total Customers
- Average Product Price and Total Quantity Sold
- Monthly Revenue and Profit Trend Analysis
- Revenue by Product Category
- Top Performing Product Categories
- Interactive KPI Cards and Filters
- Dynamic Slicers for better data exploration

### Business Questions Answered
1. How is the business performing overall?
  → Displays the overall sales and profit performance.
2. What is the monthly revenue and profit trend?
  → Tracks revenue and profit growth over time.
3. Which product categories contribute the most revenue?
  → Identifies the highest revenue-generating categories.
4. Is business performance improving over time?
  → Monitors key performance indicators at a glance.
